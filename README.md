## About Me

I'm Malavika, an aspiring **UI/UX Designer** based in Kannur, India, currently training at **Zoople**. My focus is visual communication and user-centered design — learning to turn ideas into clear, intentional, and beautiful digital experiences.

Right now I'm building my foundation in **Adobe Photoshop** and **Adobe Illustrator**, working through the fundamentals that good design is built on: layout, typography, color, and composition. Every poster and creative I make is a step toward my goal — designing thoughtful, usable interfaces that put people first.
